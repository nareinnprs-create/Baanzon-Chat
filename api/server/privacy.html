Privacy Policy — Baanzon Chat

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Baanzon Chat service (the website at https://baanzonchat.opraiz.com and the Baanzon Chat Android app) collects, uses, and protects your information. By creating an account or using the service, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. Your display name, email address, and password (stored only as a cryptographic hash). If you set a profile picture, we store that image.
  • Conversation content. The messages you send, replies you receive, and files you upload are stored so your conversation history can be shown to you and so the AI can use context in a conversation.
  • Voice input. When you use voice mode, your microphone audio is streamed to our servers while the feature is active and processed in real time to transcribe your speech and generate a spoken reply. Your audio itself is not stored; the resulting transcript becomes part of the conversation as described above.
  • Session data. A signed session cookie keeps you logged in, and your interface preferences (such as theme and settings) are stored in your browser or device storage.
  • Diagnostics. When enabled, we collect server logs and basic performance and error metrics (for example, request timing and crash reports). These diagnostics do not include the content of your conversations.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the service: authenticate you, maintain your conversation history, generate AI responses, transcribe speech, and synthesize spoken replies.
  • To operate and secure the service: prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, and keep accounts safe.
  • To communicate with you about your account (for example, verification email).

Your input is processed by the AI systems that power Baanzon Chat in order to generate responses. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Sharing

We share information only with the providers needed to run the service — for example, the infrastructure that hosts the application and database, the service that delivers transactional email, and the AI processing services operated as part of Baanzon Chat. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Security

Connections are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and access to production data is restricted to the operator.

5. Retention and your controls

We keep your data while your account is active. From the app's Settings → Data & privacy you can export your data and delete your account (password confirmation required), which removes your conversations. You may also request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Android app

The Baanzon Chat Android app is a wrapper around this same service, so the practices above apply. The app requests the microphone permission only when you use voice mode; you can revoke it at any time in your device settings. Voice activity is also indicated by an on-screen indicator while the microphone is in use.

7. Children

Baanzon Chat is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; changes take effect when posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

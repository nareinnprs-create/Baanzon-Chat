Delete your Baanzon Chat account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, yourself, in two steps.

How to delete your account

  1. Open https://baanzonchat.opraiz.com/settings (or open the app and go to Settings) and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account, confirm with your password, and confirm the deletion dialog.

Prefer email? Write to support@opraiz.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account" and we will delete it for you within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your conversations and their contents
  • Files you uploaded (images, documents)
  • Voice session transcripts and your profile (name, username, email, phone number)
  • Marketplace agents you published

What is kept, and for how long

  • Billing records (invoices, purchase references) are retained for 7 years as required by tax law. They contain no conversation content.
  • Server logs are retained for up to 90 days for security, then removed.

Partial deletion

Without deleting your account you can remove individual conversations and files from the app at any time, export your data from Settings → Data controls, and withdraw optional AI-training consent in Settings → Account.
